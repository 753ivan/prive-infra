In 2019–2021 Chat A was an ordinary group chat. After February 2022 it went silent: 6 messages in 2022, 15 in 2023. Then from November 2025 it was taken over by mass mailings: 38,696 messages by the end of September 2026 alone, with the most popular ad repeated hundreds of times.

“Unload a car – 5,000 cash, a couple of hours”

918 accountsmedian 5,000 ₽“a couple of hours” – 1,313 times“advance” – 1,687“16–17 and up” – 750
Almost half of the flow. Pay several times above market – that’s not payment for work but for the risk being shifted onto you. Ukraine’s SBU warns that Russian intelligence services pose as employers and hand out tasks with a hidden criminal purpose: deliver a package, photograph a site, set a car on fire. Drug couriers and money mules, whose cards are used to move other people’s money, are recruited the same way.
an anonymous text, above-market pay, “details in DMs”. And “16 and up” means they’re hunting minors.
02

“Tour: tickets, accommodation, safety guaranteed”

Recruiting girls. Flights and accommodation on us, complete safety, legal, high income.
916 ads in Chat A9,714 in Chat B“safety” – 519travel – 335accommodation – 179
They invite you to Hong Kong and Asia, Russia, Germany and other European countries, Dubai. Almost every second ad promises “safety”, every third a paid trip, every fifth accommodation. These three promises are exactly what most often turn into a “debt” you then have to work off. And lies about the law: one ad claims that “escorting is legal in Sweden”. In fact, buying sex has been a criminal offence there since 1999, and in Norway since 2009.
everything paid for on your behalf will later be called a debt. How to vet a “manager” before a trip – see our checklist.

2,112 messages129 accounts
Nobody vets these loans in chats. At best it’s a microloan at a predatory rate. At worst it’s harvesting your passport, a selfie with your ID and your card number, which are then used to take out loans in your name or for blackmail.
your passport and a selfie with your ID to a stranger – never.

“I’ll send my pics to whoever asks” and emoji links

These are bots: 2,016 identical messages from just 35 accounts. They lead to paid channels and phishing pages. Another 14% of the flow is messages made only of emoji with a link: that’s how they get past moderation filters. Inside it’s the same – “work”, loans, casinos.
the same text dozens of times is a mass mailing, not a person.

Georgia rarely came up in Chat A over the year, and when it did, it was a few identical ads from one account with a Ukrainian number. An unknown intermediary, a hotel and “clear money” – the full risk package, and in Georgia, messages with an intermediary are also evidence in a criminal case. More in our guide “A Winter with Light”.
unknown intermediary + “tonight” + “clear money” = don’t reply and don’t forward it to friends.

Check an ad in 10 seconds

1
The money doesn’t match the task5,000 for “a couple of hours”, $500 “clear”, “from $1,000 a week, no experience”.
2
“All details in DMs”If the terms can’t be written in the group chat, they shouldn’t be carried out either.
3
Advance, tickets, accommodation “on us”Everything paid for on your behalf will later be called a debt.
4
“Safe”, “guaranteed”, “legal”The louder the guarantee, the less is behind it. Check the country’s laws yourself.
5
“17 and up”A normal employer doesn’t look for teenagers through an anonymous chat.
6
The same text from different accountsRepeated dozens of times – so it’s a mass mailing.
If you’ve already repliedDon’t send photos of documents, video notes or your card number. Save the conversation and the username. How we counted

Two open Russian-language Telegram chats, exported in autumn 2026: Chat A – 618,561 messages since 2018, Chat B – 526,326 messages since January 2024. Messages were tagged automatically by keywords; “live” messages are texts repeated no more than three times. The figures are therefore approximate, but the orders of magnitude are reliable. We don’t publish chat names, usernames, phone numbers or links.
