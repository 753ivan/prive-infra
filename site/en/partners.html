Non-negotiable rules

The protection belongs to the model, even if the agency pays. We say this openly because models need to know: PRIVÉ is on their side.

  • The account belongs to the modelShe completes the check herself and decides what to remove. The agency doesn’t see reports, links or her data without her consent.
  • Protection leaves with the modelIf she leaves the agency, the paid period stays with her. It can’t be revoked.
  • She left — take her profiles downThe most common complaint in the chats: a girl left, but her profiles are still up and ads are running on them. A partner takes profiles down within 7 days of her leaving. Otherwise we take them down, and the partnership ends.
  • No orders “on a model”An agency can’t order removal of her content, a search for a model or information about her. Neither current nor former.
  • Commission only for a self-completed checkA model who didn’t complete verification herself doesn’t count towards the programme.
  • Signs of coercion end the partnershipIf a model can’t speak for herself or has no access to her own phone, we suspend the partnership and offer her free help and La Strada’s contacts.
