Questions

What people usually ask in the group chats

My profile is still up at my former manager’s. Can it really be removed?

Yes. A site removes a profile at the request of the account owner or the person in the photos. We confirm it’s you in the photos and write to the site. If it stays silent, we write to the host. We separately remove copies in the site’s archive and on mirrors.

Can my photos be removed from face search?

From PimEyes and similar services — yes, through their opt-out form. But they only show links; the photos themselves sit on other sites. So we remove both the service results and the sites it took them from.

Why does it work for you and not for others?

We search by face, not by image, we know the sites Google can’t see, and when a site stays silent we write to its host and registrar. Plus we know which complaint each platform responds to.

Has anyone worked with you yet?

Ask in your group chat. We don’t buy reviews or write them ourselves: what the girls say matters more to us than what we write.

Can absolutely everything be removed?

Everything on regular sites, in search engines and with regular hosts — yes. Fly-by-night sites sometimes last longer; then we remove them from search so no one finds them. Before we start, you see the list of what we’ll remove and the timeline.

What if you scam me?

The check is free; for removal, half upfront and the rest after the first removed link. SOS — after the first call. If we don’t remove what we agreed, we refund.

I’m outside the capital, my rate isn’t big-city money. Is there anything cheaper?

Yes, the Start plan at $75 a month: a monthly check and up to 5 links removed.

Do I need to show my passport?

Not to us. A video note and access to your own profile are enough. Some face-search engines ask for an ID to process removal themselves — we’ll show you how to hide what’s not needed.

Why did Google refuse my removal request?

Its form for intimate images doesn’t apply if the shoot was paid. For work photos there’s another route: copyright and platform rules.

Why is it so expensive?

A real operator does the work, not a robot, and we put our money on the result. Cheap services in this space are either automation that can’t see profile sites or a risk that your photos end up in someone else’s database.
